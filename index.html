Frequently asked questions

About these projects

Who approved these projects?

The study's leadership team reviewed proposals from collaborators and approved the projects listed on this page, checking primarily for completeness.

Why don't some projects have a preregistration or paper link yet?

Teams post their preregistrations as they finish their analysis plans, and their papers after the main paper is published. Until we've received a link, the table shows a dash (—).

When will results from these projects be available?

After the main paper has been published. Teams hold secondary results, including preprints, talks, and posts, until then to protect the Registered Report review at Nature. We'll link each paper or preprint in the table.

Can I get the data?

We will aim to make the data publicly available, along with a paper detailing its data structure, in 2027. Collaborators who worked on the GSME will receive access to the data before we release it publicly.

How should I cite the study?

Cite the Stage 1 Registered Report: Rathje, S.*, Asimovic, N.*, Ventura, T.*, Mughal, S., Karsting, H., Barrie, C., DeVerna, M., Robertson, C. E., The Global Social Media Experiment Team, Tucker, J. A., & Van Bavel, J. J. (Stage 1 In Principle Acceptance of Registered Report). Testing the causal impact of social media usage around the globe. Nature. *Co-first authors. The preprint is available on PsyArXiv. We'll update this citation once the paper is published.

For GSME Collaborators

I am a collaborator for the GSME. Where can I read the full details about the procedure for getting early data access?

The full procedure is in the Manual for Country Teams, which can be found in the shared folder "GSME - Public Docs."

My project is missing. What should I do?

If it has been 10 days since you submitted your materials and they are still not on the site, please email us at globalsocialmediastudy@gmail.com.

How do I add my project?

We shared a protocol for this with all GSME collaborators. If you did not receive it, please double-check your email and check in with your team leader.

Do I need a preregistration?

To receive early access to the global data, you need a preregistration. After our main paper is published, we will make the dataset publicly available, and no preregistration will be required.

Can I share the data with students or colleagues?

Only with people working on your approved project. Don't post or redistribute the data.

When can I publish or present my results?

Please wait until the main paper has been published before sharing secondary results publicly, including preprints, talks, and posts. This protects the Registered Report review at Nature. You're free to analyze the data and write up your work in the meantime.

Another project looks similar to mine. Is that a problem?

The leadership team reviewed overlap when approving projects. If you think your work overlaps with another team's, let us know and we'll put you in touch.

Still have questions?

Email the leadership team at globalsocialmediastudy@gmail.com. We read every message, though it may take a few days to reply.

The Global Social Media Experiment is an international collaboration of more than 200 researchers. Learn more at globalsocialmediastudy.com.
